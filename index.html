How it works

  1. Add the itinerary. In JetInsight open it, tap Share → Clipper Fuel Planner, then tap ADD ITINERARY. Or tap Add itinerary below and pick the PDF.
  2. Pick the day. Legs from different itineraries on the same date and tail are merged.
  3. Enter fuel on board. The plan appears right away: what to add before departure and what to buy at each stop to get the fees waived.
  4. Winds are optional. Each leg's burn comes from the King Air 350 profile in Aircraft settings (taxi + climb + cruise by distance + descent), still air by default. Tap Get forecast winds in the plan to pull the GFS forecast for each leg (FL260 westbound / FL250 eastbound) and recalculate; Remove winds goes back. Under Edit legs you can also type a wind by hand or the burn from the filed flight plan.

Extra fuel goes to:

    Edit legs

    Block: type 105 for 1:05. Quick turn skips parking fees; Overnight adds them. Changing a Buy amount here works the same as tapping it in the plan.
    Dep is the local departure time (from the itinerary) used to pick the forecast hour. Wind overrides the forecast: + tailwind, − headwind, in knots. Flight-plan burn overrides everything for that leg; use it once the flight plan is filed. Distance comes from the itinerary (or great circle); type over it if the routing is longer. FL overrides the cruise level used for the forecast.

    Aircraft settings
    Burn profile per leg

    King Air 350 defaults. Changes stay on this phone. Each leg burns taxi + climb + cruise + descent, where cruise = (distance − climb − descent distance) ÷ groundspeed × cruise burn, then the contingency is added to the airborne part. Legs shorter than climb + descent distance scale those two phases down. Distance comes from the itinerary or the airport table; the fallback rate is used only when it is unknown. Winds change the cruise groundspeed. Westbound = magnetic course 180–359°.
